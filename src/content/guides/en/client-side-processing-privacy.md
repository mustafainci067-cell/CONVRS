## The Old Model: Server-Side Processing

Traditionally, if you wanted to convert a PDF or compress an image, you had to upload it to a server. The server processed the file and sent it back. This exposed your data to third parties.

## The Rise of WebAssembly (Wasm)

WebAssembly allows languages like C, C++, and Rust to run in the browser at near-native speeds. This changed everything.

## Zero-Backend Architecture

Our platform uses zero-backend architecture. When you upload a file, it never leaves your browser. FFmpeg and pdf-lib run directly on your device CPU.

## Benefits of Client-Side Processing

The benefits are immense: absolute privacy, faster processing (no upload/download wait times), and reduced server costs for developers.

## The Future of the Web

As browsers get more powerful, we will see even more complex applications moving completely to the client side, putting privacy back in the hands of the user.
