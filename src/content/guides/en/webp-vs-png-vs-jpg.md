## The Big Three

Images make up a huge portion of web page weight. Choosing the right format is critical for Core Web Vitals.

## JPEG (JPG)

Developed in the 90s, JPEG is lossy and best suited for complex photographs with many colors. It does not support transparency.

## PNG

PNG is a lossless format that supports transparency (alpha channel). It is perfect for logos, icons, and illustrations with flat colors.

## WebP

Developed by Google, WebP supports both lossy and lossless compression, as well as transparency and animation. It generally produces files 25-30% smaller than JPEGs.

## SEO Impact

Google specifically recommends serving images in next-gen formats like WebP. Smaller files mean faster load times and better rankings.
